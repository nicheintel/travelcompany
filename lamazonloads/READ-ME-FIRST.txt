LamazonLoads - how to test the website on your Windows computer
================================================================

You need XAMPP installed (https://www.apachefriends.org) - PHP 8.1 or newer.

1. Download / extract this folder anywhere (for example your Desktop).
2. Open the "lamazonloads" folder.
3. Double-click  START-LamazonLoads.bat

That's it. The website opens in your browser at:
   http://localhost/lamazonloads/

FIRST THING TO DO: click "Get loaded" and create your own account.
The first account created on your computer becomes the ADMIN.
You'll then see "Admin" in the top menu, where you can:
  - add, edit, close or delete job posts
  - see every applicant, their driver profile and documents
  - change application status (Received / In review / Approved / Not selected)
  - read messages sent from the Contact page
  - make other team members admins, or reset a member's password

To start it again later, double-click START-LamazonLoads.bat again.
To stop it, double-click STOP-LamazonLoads.bat.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
"Run anyway". You can open the .bat file in Notepad to see exactly what it does.

Your contact email / phone (shown on the website) go in:
   C:\xampp\htdocs\lamazonloads\config.local.php
Open it in Notepad, remove the // in front of a line and fill it in.

Logo files (for social media, business cards, email signatures) are in
the "brand" folder.
